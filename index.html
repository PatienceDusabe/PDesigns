YOUR MEASUREMENTS

Your measurements, made simple.

You don't need to visit a tailor just to provide your measurements. You can measure yourself at home using a flexible measuring tape.

Use centimetres (cm) and keep the tape comfortably against your body without pulling it too tightly.

Measurements we need

Height

Measure from the top of your head to the floor while standing straight.

Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest or bust.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Shoulder

Measure from one shoulder point to the other.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the shoulder point down to where you want the sleeve to end.

Garment Length

Measure from the desired starting point to where you want the garment to end.
